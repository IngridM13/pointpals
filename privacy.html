Privacy & ads

Your votes: PointPals has no backend. Names, avatars and votes travel directly between the browsers in a room (WebRTC, brokered by the free PeerJS signalling service, which sees connection metadata such as IP addresses). We do not store them.

On your device: we save your name, avatar, sound setting and ad-consent choice in your browser's local storage.

Ads: PointPals is free thanks to ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show personalised ads if you accept, or non-personalised ads if you choose that option. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services and manage your choices at Ads Settings.
